So, what's a settai?

sēṭṭai (சேட்டை)

noun, Tamil. Say it like you mean trouble.

  1. Mischief. The playful kind that makes everyone laugh, including the victim, eventually. "Who hid the office stapler again? Settai."
  2. The energy in a room right before Siva walks in. "Things were calm. Then the settai arrived."
  3. A leadership style. Build a team that trusts each other enough to joke, and they'll build anything. "Our standups got shorter and louder. Very settai."
